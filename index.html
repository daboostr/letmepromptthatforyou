How Let Me Prompt That For You works

  1. Paste the question a coworker asked you, the one they could have asked Copilot.
  2. Share the link in Teams, Slack or email.
  3. They watch an animation that types their question into Copilot and presses send, then they land in Microsoft Copilot with the answer.

Frequently asked questions

What is Let Me Prompt That For You?

It is the AI version of Let Me Google That For You. Paste a question someone asked you, share the link, and they watch an animation that types their question into Microsoft Copilot before sending them there.

Is Let Me Copilot That For You free?

Yes. It is free, needs no sign-up, and runs entirely in your browser. Questions are only stored in the link you share.

Will it support Gemini or ChatGPT?

Copilot is the first supported assistant. A Gemini version is coming soon.
